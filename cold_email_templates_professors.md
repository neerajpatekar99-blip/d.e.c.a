## 📬 Strategic Protocol: How to Send These Emails

1. **Send Time:** Send on **Tuesday or Wednesday morning between 9:15 AM and 10:30 AM IST**. Professors check and clear their inboxes at the start of their lab day.
2. **Subject Line:** Keep it under 9 words. Academics ignore marketing buzzwords; they open specific, polite, and research-focused subject lines.
3. **No Large File Attachments:** Link directly to your GitHub repository or project documentation. Busy professors rarely download unknown `.zip` or `.pdf` attachments from student emails.
4. **Follow-Up Protocol:** If there is no reply after **6 working days**, send a polite 2-sentence follow-up on the same email thread.
