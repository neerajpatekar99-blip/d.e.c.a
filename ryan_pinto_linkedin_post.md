> **Balancing 10th Boards and Clean Nuclear Energy: A Ryanite’s Journey into Frontier Science.** ⚛️📚
>
> Mentors and teachers have always told us: *"Don't just study science to pass exams—use science to solve humanity's greatest challenges."*
>
> Alongside my Class 10 Maharashtra SSC Board preparation at **Ryan International School, Kalamboli**, I have spent my late evenings working on **Project D.E.C.A.** (**D**irect **E**lectromagnetic **C**onversion **A**rchitecture)—a theoretical clean energy framework designed to solve nuclear energy’s 140-year-old mechanical bottleneck: boiling water to spin steam turbines.
>
> ⚡ **The Vision Behind D.E.C.A.:**  
> While traditional power plants waste up to 65% of their energy as rejected thermal heat, D.E.C.A. couples an aneutronic **Dense Plasma Focus (DPF)** with a multi-stage **Faraday Induction Stator**. By firing a high-speed magnetized plasmoid (387 km/s) through liquid-nitrogen-cooled copper coils, the system directly extracts electricity at a modeled **69.5% kinetic-to-electric efficiency**—with zero steam, zero turbines, and zero radioactive waste (producing only safe, inert Helium-4 alpha particles).
>
> 🎓 **My Next Milestone:**  
> My dream after cracking my Class 10 Boards is to pursue a **Diploma in Chemical Engineering at VJTI Mumbai**, diving deeper into high-temperature materials, decaborane fuel sublimation kinetics, and pulsed-power systems.
